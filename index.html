The three things that catch people

You probably don't need a Mexican marriage licence

The US State Department itself notes that couples often marry legally at home and hold the ceremony abroad, avoiding foreign-certificate complexity entirely. Most resort weddings in Mexico are symbolic. That is not a lesser wedding — the paperwork just happened somewhere cheaper.

The decision, laid out →

The blood test happens after you land

Quintana Roo and Baja California Sur both require pre-marital lab work done in Mexico, valid only 15 days. It cannot be done at home in advance. That single rule is why legal ceremonies need days in-country before the wedding.

State-by-state requirements →

Your US certified translation may not count

Mexican civil registries generally want a perito traductor authorised in Mexico — in Baja California Sur, authorised by the state's own high court. The apostille has to be translated too. Canadians should start three-plus months out.
