Contact

Ask me anything

One address, read by one person. Questions are answered free whether or not you ever buy anything, in English or Spanish — escríbeme en español si prefieres.

hello@thevowkit.com

No contact form, no chat widget, no newsletter signup, no autoresponder sequence. Your address is used to reply to you and nothing else.

Start with one of these if it's easier

Each opens your email app with the subject filled in. Delete whatever doesn't apply.

  • “Should we marry legally in Mexico or at home?” — the decision the whole plan hangs on. Tell me your state, your date and where you live.
  • “Is our document timeline actually going to work?” — apostilles, translations, and the blood-test window. Canadians especially: ask early, GAC has no rush option.
  • “Which of the two program booklets do we need?” — worth asking before you buy rather than after. Paste what your parish told you.
  • “An office told us something different from your page.” — please send it. Your fresh answer beats my citation, and I'd rather publish the correction than defend a stale page.
  • Anything about the kits — including whether you should skip them. The reference page is free and complete; if it covers what you need, I'll say so.

What to expect

  • A real reply from a person, usually within a couple of days. This is a one-person operation in Los Cabos, so it is not instant, and there is nobody to escalate to.
  • Answers that cite something, or say plainly that I don't know. If your question turns on a municipality I haven't verified, that is the answer you'll get, along with what to ask the office and how to word it in Spanish.
  • No sales sequence. Asking a question does not put you on a list. There is no list.
  • “Don't buy it” is a normal answer here. A refund costs us both more than an honest reply does.

What I can't do

I am not a lawyer, not a notario, and not a Registro Civil official — see About for the full extent of the credential. I can tell you what a state's own page says, what it means in practice, and which question to put to your municipal office. I cannot give legal advice, file anything on your behalf, or overrule the office that will actually marry you.

Before you write, this may already answer it

The Mexico marriage requirements reference is free and covers civil versus symbolic, the four main states, apostille and translation lead times, the 15-day blood-test trap, and full document checklists — each with its source and check date. If your question is in there, you'll have the answer faster than I can send it.
